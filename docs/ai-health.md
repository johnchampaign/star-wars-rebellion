### Notable

Two hunt regimes stay clearly split, and the split is exactly the known one.
In *self-play* (consolidation bench, thin relocating AI defender) the Empire
does fine — 52% overall, 49% reveal rate, 76% of reveals converted. But
against the *human-like hold-defender* in the smoke replays, the AI finds the
base in **0/17** recorded positions. That is the whole story of the current AI:
strong once it finds the base, unable to find it against a defender who holds
and garrisons rather than relocating on contact. The planner (#539) is doing
its job post-reveal (conversion 78.7% → 91.0% with it on); the value is gated
behind a hunt that can't reach a real defender's base. Nothing regressed this
week — this is the baseline everything else will be measured against.
